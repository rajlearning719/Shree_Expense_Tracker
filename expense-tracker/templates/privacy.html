Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("the Service") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password (stored securely in hashed form).
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic usage information — such as login activity, used to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to operate and improve the Service. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Store and display your expenses so you can track your spending.
  • Generate summaries and category breakdowns for your own use.

We do not sell, rent, or use your personal financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a private database used only to power the Service. We take reasonable measures to protect it, including hashing passwords and restricting access. You retain full ownership of your data and may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Please note that no system is completely secure, so you use the Service at your own risk.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly is designed to keep your data in-house. We do not share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. Any limited third-party tools we may use (for example, to load fonts or host the application) are used only to deliver the Service and are not given access to your expense records.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the app's support channel.
